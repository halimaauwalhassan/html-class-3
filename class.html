Defected Lawmakers Were Suspended Long Before Leaving APC — Zamfara Speaker

According to a statement issued by the Speaker, the lawmakers were also accused of breaking into the offices of the Clerk and the Sergeant-at-Arms, forcibly entering the legislative chamber and damaging public property before holding an unauthorised sitting.

The Speaker of the Zamfara State House of Assembly, Bilyaminu Ismail Moriki, has said that the eight lawmakers who recently announced their defection from the All Progressives Congress (APC) had been suspended long before they decided to leave the party.

Moriki said the lawmakers were suspended on February 26, 2024, over allegations of gross misconduct, conspiracy, mischief, contempt of the House and convening an unauthorised sitting in violation of the Assembly’s Standing Orders. According to a statement issued by the Speaker, the lawmakers were also accused of breaking into the offices of the Clerk and the Sergeant-at-Arms, forcibly entering the legislative chamber and damaging public property before holding an unauthorised sitting. The allegations subsequently led to the institution of a criminal complaint against the affected lawmakers before a court of competent jurisdiction.
